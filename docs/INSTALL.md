# Installing Journal (Android)

Journal is a personal app, not published on the Play Store, so Android will
show a couple of warning screens during install and sign-in. That's expected —
follow the steps below.

## 1. Download the app

Download the APK file:

https://github.com/bobtheman/Journal/raw/main/Journal/Releases/Latest

Open the link on your Android phone (e.g. tap it in Chrome, or send it to
yourself and open it from Files/Downloads).

## 2. Install it

Android will warn you because the app isn't from the Play Store:

- If asked to allow installs from this source, tap **Settings** → turn on
  **Allow from this source** → go back and tap **Install** again.
- Google Play Protect may say it can't verify the app is safe.
  Tap **Install anyway** (or **More details** → **Install anyway**).

## 3. Sign in with Google

The first time you open the app and sign in with Google:

- You'll see a screen saying **"Google hasn't verified this app"**.
  This is normal — the app is still in Google's testing/review stage.
  Tap **Continue** to proceed.
- **Your Google account must be added to the test user list first**, or
  sign-in will fail with an access error. If you haven't been added yet,
  ask the app owner to add your Gmail address, then try signing in again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "App not installed" | Delete any older copy of Journal first, then reinstall. |
| Sign-in fails with an access/403 error | Your Google account isn't whitelisted yet — ask the app owner to add it. |
| Can't find the downloaded APK | Check your browser's Downloads folder or notification. |
